# Getting started

STEWARD is an admin cockpit for managing **Clients** (branded organizations),
their **Customers** (individuals or households), and **Task Tickets** — with
branded document generation.

## Concepts

- **Client** — a branded organization. Owns the logo, colors and company info
  stamped on generated documents.
- **Customer** — an individual or household belonging to a Client. May be joint.
- **Ticket** — a task about a Customer. Renders to a branded document.

## Demo mode

Open **Settings → Demo mode → Reset demo data** to load fictional sample
records. Demo data lives in a separate database; your real data is untouched.
